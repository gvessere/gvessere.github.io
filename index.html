About Me

I am an AI Research Engineer with broad industry experience. Over the past two decades, my work has focused on scientific computing, training AI models, and researching and implementing efficient solutions to practical problems.

My background is in applying machine learning methods to engineering problems. My main focus has been applying these methods to DNA sequencing systems at Illumina, Inc. and Element Biosciences, two industry leaders in genomics. Some of the inventions I have worked on are listed below.

With more time on my hands, I recently began a series of articles sharing my explorations as an independent researcher. Much of my interest in recent years has centered on language models, so the series naturally follows that interest. My aim is to explore and characterize their inner workings, understand them better, and possibly find new ways to improve the state of the art.
