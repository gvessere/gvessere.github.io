About Me

I am an AI Research Engineer with broad industry experience. Over the past two decades, my work has focused on scientific computing, training AI models, and researching and implementing efficient solutions to practical problems.

My background is in applying machine learning methods to engineering problems. My main focus has been applying these methods to DNA sequencing systems at Illumina, Inc. and Element Biosciences, two industry leaders in genomics. The published inventions I have worked on are listed below.

Much of my interest in recent years has centered on language models, so I am starting a series of technical notes on my explorations of their inner workings. Most of what I have explored is not presented here; these notes bring together interesting things I have found along the way. My aim is to understand the technology better and possibly find new ways to improve the state of the art.
